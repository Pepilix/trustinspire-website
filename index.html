Smart homes, electrical work and care home WiFi.

Since 2009 we have designed and installed technology in demanding homes. That experience now sits behind three specialist services.

  • 01 Smart Homes Home cinema, lighting control, audio and automation for private homes. inspire-av.com
  • 02 Electrical From 2027 Electrical contracting for new builds, renovations and high-end homes. inspire-electrical.com
  • 03 Care Home WiFi WiFi and network infrastructure designed, installed and supported for care homes. carehomewifi.com

We started in 2009, wiring and programming smart homes. Most of what we know, we learned on site.

Inspire Audio Visual built its reputation on technically demanding smart home, lighting and cinema installations, the kind where the cabling, programming and finish all have to be right, and where the client expects us to still be around years later.

That work took us into network infrastructure, and from there into care homes, which need dependable WiFi for residents, staff and care systems. Care Home WiFi now handles that side. Inspire Electrical is the newest part: electrical contracting alongside the technology work, opening in 2027.
